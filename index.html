About the Dataset

Overview & Research Scope

An academic benchmark tailored to address challenges in cultural artifact representation, fine-grained visual classification, and content retrieval.

The Moroccan Arts and Crafts Dataset (MORART-1K) comprises a balanced collection of 1,600 high-resolution images categorized into 16 distinctive classes. Meticulously assembled to address domain-specific computer vision tasks, MORART facilitates Content-Based Image Retrieval (CBIR), object recognition, visual similarity clustering, and the computational safeguarding of North African intangible cultural heritage.

The images were gathered across diverse regions of Morocco, emphasizing historic artisanal hubs such as Fez, Marrakech, Rabat, Ouarzazate, and Souss. The collection encompasses artifacts photographed in traditional artisan workshops, cultural museums, open-air souks, and ancient architectural monuments.

To ensure robust algorithmic generalizability, samples were documented under diverse real-world lighting conditions, varied perspective angles, and dynamic backgrounds, simulating challenging real-world retrieval scenarios.

  • Balanced Class Distribution

    100 images per core class ensuring unbiased classification and retrieval benchmarking.

  • Diverse Multi-Context Capture

    Photographed across natural daylight, workshop interiors, indoor lighting, and varying zoom factors.

  • Open Access & Standard Formats

    Adheres to open research FAIR principles and readily interfaces with PyTorch, TensorFlow, and Hugging Face.

# Load MORART-1K with PyTorch torchvision
import torchvision.datasets as datasets
import torchvision.transforms as transforms
from torch.utils.data import DataLoader

transform = transforms.Compose([
    transforms.Resize((256, 256)),
    transforms.CenterCrop(224),
    transforms.ToTensor(),
    transforms.Normalize(mean=[0.485, 0.456, 0.406],
                         std=[0.229, 0.224, 0.225])
])

# Extracted dataset directory structured by class folders
dataset = datasets.ImageFolder(root='path/to/morart-1k/', transform=transform)
dataloader = DataLoader(dataset, batch_size=32, shuffle=True, num_workers=4)

print(f"Loaded {len(dataset)} images across {len(dataset.classes)} Moroccan classes.")
